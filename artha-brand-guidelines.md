## 1. Brand Concept & Cultural Roots

### The Sanskrit Etymology of Artha (अर्थ)
In ancient Indian thought, **Artha** represents one of the four essential goals of human life (*Puruṣārthas*): material prosperity, financial security, and economic clarity. 

For modern tech founders, engineers, and creators, **Artha Financial Telemetry** removes the mental tax of manual expense tracking:
* **The Promise**: *"Track every rupee from WhatsApp without opening a spreadsheet."*
* **Conversational Speed**: Sub-100ms NLP processing via official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API v21.0.
* **Accounting Rigor**: Automated double-entry reconciliation, non-income split tracking, and Safe-to-Spend™ cash runway forecasting.

## 2. The Master Mark: "The Artha Glyph" (अ-Vector)

The approved identity is a bespoke, mathematically constructed Devanagari **"अ"** letterform mark:
1. **The Shirorekha (Top Roof Bar)**: Horizontal architectural beam grounding the identity.
2. **The Purna Danda (Vertical Stem)**: The upright financial pillar representing double-entry accounting integrity.
3. **Compound Growth Arcs**: Smooth left dual counter-curves tracing forward momentum.
4. **Emerald Telemetry Pulse (`#10B981`)**: The live radar beacon at the upper right signifying 24/7 WhatsApp bot uptime and healthy liquidity.
